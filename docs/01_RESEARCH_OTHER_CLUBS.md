# What other gaming clubs' apps do (research, 2026-11-14)

Mostly vendor docs and app-store pages, so these are vendor claims. Full sources are in the links.

## Apps looked at
- **IZI / iZi.Play** ([docs](https://izi.is/wiki/en/mobile/app-overview/)): balance and bonuses, booking (time → zone → seat, list by default,
  map if the club uploads one), busy seats show "Available from HH:MM", private rooms released 10 min after a no-show,
  QR at the PC starts the session, "switch device", bar orders during a session, promo campaigns.
- **LANGAME** (iOS 4.8★, ~2.3k ratings): club finder, booking calculator, promo codes, cashback, ladder; supports Bulgarian.
  Off-the-shelf option. Complaints: separate registration, refunds only at the club.
- **Colizeum (Russian chain)**: appears to share LANGAME's code. Notable: "suggest the next free slot", welcome promo code.
- **SmartShell SmartGamer**: map with free seats + PC specs, group booking (one payer, friends get a PIN), leaderboards.
- **HandyCafe Gamer**: SMS login, QR start, staff-approved reservations, levels, daily streak, push categories + quiet hours, queue.
- **ggLeap / ggMobile**: book several PCs for a time window, optional prepayment, staff lock / no-show release, one-time password.
- **Picaplay (Korea)**: live booking, remaining time, QR login. Complaints: outages with no updates.
- SENET, Gizmo, iCafeCloud, Smartlaunch: little or no phone app for players.

## Off-peak discounts
- **No vendor documents "PCs are empty → push a discount automatically".** Doing it would set Cyber Colizeum apart.
- Closest: IZI's playbook ([link](https://izi.is/wiki/en/playbooks/fill-off-peak/)) = weekdays 10:00–17:00 top-ups get +20% bonus
  (once a day, expires in 60 days) + a 9:30 weekday push to people who came in the last 30 days but not this week.
- Scheduled happy hours / night discounts / weekday bonuses are common (iCafeCloud).
- HandyCafe argues **bonus credit keeps customers better than a lower hourly price**.

## Ideas to use (ranked for 29 seats)
1. Live availability per room; busy seats show "free from HH:MM".
2. Book a specific PC; auto-release after 10–15 min no-show; suggest the next free slot.
3. Off-peak bonus (time window).
4. Occupancy-triggered flash push (our own idea) — max 1 a day, quiet hours, opt-in per category.
5. QR / one-time code login at the PC (needs the club software link).
6. Group booking: whole VIP room in one go.
7. "Tell me when it's free" waitlist.
8. Simple loyalty: a few cashback levels, daily streak, bonus kept apart from paid credit.
9. Food and drink to the seat during a session.
10. Skip for now: clans, feeds, global ladders.

## Complaints to avoid
Separate app + club logins · refunds only at the club · outages with no notice · hidden penalties ·
discounts that only work for app bookings.
